What this site collects

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies, stores no personal data, and does not follow you to other websites. What we see is how many people opened a page and roughly which country they were in. We cannot identify you from it.

We also count how many people tap the WhatsApp button, the phone number, the email address, or the map link. That tells us whether the site is doing its job. It records that a tap happened, never who tapped.

What this site does not collect

There is no contact form, no account, and no mailing list. We collect no names, email addresses or phone numbers through this website. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers, because there is nothing to sell or share.

Cookies

This site sets none. That is also why you are not being asked to dismiss a cookie banner.

When you get in touch

Tapping WhatsApp, the phone number or the email address takes you off this site. What happens after that is covered by WhatsApp's or your email provider's own terms, not ours. Anything you send us directly we keep only for as long as we are doing business together, and we do not pass it on.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it. Email goldenrainmarket@gmail.com or call +968 9932 0963 and we will answer within thirty days.

Changes

If what we collect changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. We will not quietly start collecting more than is described here.
