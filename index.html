Silal · Khazaen Economic City, Barka

We don't buy at the market. We work inside it.

Our unit is inside Silal, Oman's central fruit and vegetable market. First pick, every morning. Straight from the floor to our cold room.

Silal opened in 2024. Before it, Al Mawaleh, for nearly three decades. We moved with the market.

Importers, wholesalers, customs and the labs, all under one roof. A whole day saved.

Your trusted distributor of fresh fruits and vegetables across Oman.

The cold chain

Once it is cold,
