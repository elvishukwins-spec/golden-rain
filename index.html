Silal · Khazaen Economic City, Barka

We don't buy at the market. We work inside it.

Our unit sits within Silal, Oman's central fruit and vegetable market. We have direct access and first pick, and nothing sits between the trading floor and our cold room. It is why the produce that reaches you is a day fresher than it has any right to be.

Silal opened in 2024 and replaced Al Mawaleh as the Sultanate's produce hub. We traded through the old market for most of three decades, and moved with it.

  1. 01Lands Trucks reverse into the receiving bays. Nothing has left the site yet.
  2. 02Inspected Customs, agricultural quarantine and the food-safety lab sit on the same floor plan.
  3. 03Bought Our unit is inside the hall, so nothing is driven anywhere and nobody handles it twice.
  4. 04Cold Straight into our own room, still inside the building it landed in.

Silal consolidates importers, exporters, wholesalers, customs and laboratory facilities in one destination. That is where the day comes off the clock.

Your trusted distributor of fresh fruits and vegetables across Oman.

Bought by eye, not by list.

The cold chain

Sealed at the market.
Not opened again
until it reaches you.

Hand graded, inspected and packed for table-ready arrival, then moved in a refrigerated fleet built for daily, multi-region delivery. Their phrase for it: farm-fresh to table-ready.

Freshness

Fresh is not a claim.
It is a schedule.

One day, in order.

Everything below happens between the market opening and your kitchen door.

  1. 04:00

    On the floor at Silal

    Bought by eye at the trading hall in Barka, before most of the country is awake.

  2. 05:40

    Hand graded

    Checked and packed crate by crate. If it is not at its peak it does not go on the truck.

  3. 06:45

    Into the cold room

    The chain closes here and does not open again until it reaches you.

  4. 07:15

    Backed into the bay

    Loaded and gone before the heat. Bay CSE 30, every morning.

  5. 09:00

    On the shelf

    Stacked and faced in the shop before the first customer walks in. This is where our name stops being on the box.

  6. 19:30

    On the plate

    Fifteen hours from the market floor to a table somewhere in the Sultanate. That is the whole job.
